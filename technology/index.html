How we work

We start with your process, not a product.

Large firms begin with analysis before they recommend anything. We do the same, for owner-led businesses at every stage.

  1. 1

    Map the current process

    We sit with your team and document how work actually flows today: the tools, the handoffs, the spreadsheets and the workarounds.

  2. 2

    Find where technology helps

    We rank the opportunities by effort and payoff, so you see what to fix first and what can wait.

  3. 3

    Choose and negotiate

    We compare the options, including our own, and negotiate with outside vendors on your behalf.

  4. 4

    Build, connect and run

    We implement the solution, train your people, and stay on to manage it for as long as you want us to.
