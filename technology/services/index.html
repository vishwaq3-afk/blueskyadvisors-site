Cybersecurity, honestly

What we do, and what we do not promise.

No one can promise you will never be attacked. We focus on the work that measurably lowers your risk, and we say plainly where a specialist is the better choice.

What we do

  • Assess your current security basics and rank the gaps
  • Harden accounts, email, devices and backups
  • Write simple policies and an incident plan your team can follow
  • Help you choose security tools and vendors, and negotiate the price

What we do not do

  • Guarantee that a breach will not happen
  • Provide 24/7 monitoring or emergency incident response ourselves
  • Perform penetration testing or forensic investigations
  • Recommend a product because it suits us rather than because it suits you

When you need monitoring, response or testing, we will point you to a qualified specialist and help you evaluate the proposal.
