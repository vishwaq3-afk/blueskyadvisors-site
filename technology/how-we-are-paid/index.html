Transparency

How we are paid. So you can trust what we recommend.

Advice is only worth something if you know who benefits from it. Here is exactly how money flows to us, and the rules we follow when we recommend a tool.

The basics

Two ways we earn money.

1. Fees from you

You pay us for our time and our work. The scope and the fee are agreed in writing before we start. This is our main source of income.

2. Commissions from some vendors

Some software companies pay us a commission when someone signs up through our referral link. The commission comes from the vendor. It does not raise the price you pay.

Our rules

What we promise when we recommend a tool.

  1. Fit comes first.We recommend the tool that suits your business, even when it pays us nothing or pays someone else.
  2. We tell you every time.When a recommendation includes our link, we say so right next to it, in our proposals and in our messages.
  3. You can skip our link.You are free to sign up directly with any vendor. Just ask and we will tell you how.
  4. We name the alternatives.When a better option exists, we say so, whether or not we earn anything from it.
  5. We describe the relationship accurately.We call ourselves an affiliate when we are one, and a partner only when the company has accepted us as one.

Questions about how we work?

Ask us anything about who pays us and why we recommend what we do. We will give you a straight answer.
