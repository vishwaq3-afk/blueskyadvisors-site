About us

People who have been through it, teaching what they learned.

Bluesky Advisors was started by former and current senior leaders who have worked through the business, financial and technology landscape from the inside. That experience is now available to individuals, owners and teams. Our purpose is literacy and education: helping you understand your options clearly before you decide.

Our purpose

Education comes first.

Big decisions about money, a business or a career are often made with half the picture, because nobody ever taught the rules or the vocabulary.

We start by teaching. We explain how things work, what the trade-offs are and which questions are worth asking. We say plainly what we know and where our experience ends. Then the decision is yours.

Who we are

Leaders who have done the work.

Bluesky Advisors is led by former and current senior leaders. We bring operating experience, not theory, from the organizations where these decisions are made every day.

Around that core is a small circle of practitioners who share one commitment: teach first, advise second.

What we do

Four ways we help.

Finance

Individuals and families

Financial literacy and protection planning for people and households, explained step by step so you know what each choice does before you make it.

Business

Small business owners

Practical guidance on the decisions that keep a small business steady, in plain language you can act on, without needing to hire a department to understand it.

Technology

Technical advisory

Advice on enterprise technology, vendor and workforce programs, data and AI, drawn from hands-on experience inside large organizations.

Career

Life coaching

Structured conversations to help you weigh a career move, a transition or a long-term goal, and turn it into a plan you believe in.

How we work

Plain language

Your decision

We lay out the options and the trade-offs. The choice, and the pace, belong to you.
